**Dear Faithful of St. Mary,**

A warm reminder that our Adult Bible Study continues this week, facilitated by Hayr Eremia.

Our first week was a great success, with strong parishioner participation. Thank you to everyone who came, asked questions, and shared in the discussion.

This second week we will begin with a focus on Matthew, Chapter 1.

These weekly gatherings remain an ongoing exploration of Holy Scripture — a chance to dig deeper into the living Word of God, ask questions, share insights, and grow together in faith. Sessions last 1 to 1½ hours and take place at St. Mary Armenian Church.

Two class options are available:

-   **Monday Evenings – 7:00 pm**  
    Ideal for working adults and those with daytime commitments.  
    **This week: Monday, August 31**
-   **Wednesday Mornings – 10:30 am**  
    Especially suited for retirees and anyone who prefers not to drive at night.  
    **This week: Wednesday, September 2**

For the Wednesday morning class, we are happy to help coordinate carpools. Simply let us know when you RSVP and we will do our best to connect you with others.

You are welcome to simply show up, but an RSVP is greatly appreciated so we can plan accordingly. Please reply to this email, write to [info@stmaryarmenianchurch.com](mailto:info@stmaryarmenianchurch.com), or leave a message with the church office at (949) 650-8367.

Questions? Feel free to contact the church office or send us an email.

Whether you joined us last week or this will be your first session, you are warmly invited. Come as you are — bring your curiosity, your questions, and an open heart. We look forward to continuing to learn and grow together.

In Christ’s love, St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church
