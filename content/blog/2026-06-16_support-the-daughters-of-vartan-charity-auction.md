Dear St. Mary Parishioners and Friends,

The **Daughters of Vartan** invite our parish community to participate in a special charity auction featuring a beautifully handcrafted **Sayat Nova Tavloo (backgammon board)**. This unique work of Armenian craftsmanship celebrates the legacy of Sayat Nova and offers an opportunity to support a meaningful cause.

All proceeds from the auction will benefit the **Armenian Lighthouse Charitable Foundation**, helping to support its important humanitarian and community programs.

**Auction Information**  
• Online bidding is now open  
• Auction closes on **June 17, 2026 (at midnight)**  
• Those who wish to support the cause without bidding may also make a donation through the "Pledge Donate" option. Donations are separate from auction bids.

Additional photos, videos, and assistance are available by contacting:  
**[DOVAnoush@gmail.com](mailto:DOVAnoush@gmail.com)**

We encourage everyone to share this opportunity with family and friends. Whether you place a bid or make a donation, your participation helps support a worthy Armenian charitable organization.

Thank you for supporting the efforts of the Daughters of Vartan and for your continued commitment to serving our Armenian community.

With gratitude,

**St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church**  
Parish Council
