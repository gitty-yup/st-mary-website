Love & Homeland — This Saturday!

This Saturday, October 24

Join Us for an Evening of Armenian Song

AGBU LA Choir

Love & Homeland

Featuring the OC Saturday School Children’s Choir

Dear St. Mary Parish Family,

The **AGBU Orange County Chapter** invites all parishioners of St. Mary to a beautiful evening of Armenian music **this Saturday** in our own Stambolian Hall. The AGBU LA Choir, under the direction of Nektar Mirakyan, will present _Love & Homeland_, joined by the OC Saturday School Children’s Choir and a musical ensemble led by Shushan Hakobyan.

Proceeds benefit the **Armenian Genocide Memorial** at the Orange County Great Park. Let’s fill the hall and support this important cause together.

Saturday, October 24, 2026  |  7:00 p.m.

**Stambolian Hall**  
St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church  
148 E. 22nd Street, Costa Mesa, CA 92626

**Choir Conductor:** Nektar Mirakyan  
**Musical Ensemble** led by Shushan Hakobyan  
**Featuring:** OC Saturday School Children’s Choir

How to Purchase Tickets

**Online (now):** Purchase full-price adult tickets ($60) only. Scan the QR code on the attached flyer.

**At the door (Saturday):** All discounted and youth tickets must be purchased at the door:

• College & High School students: $30  
• ACYO members: $10  
• Children under 12: Free

_Recommendation: Buy adult tickets online in advance, then add any youth or discounted tickets at the door on Saturday._

Bring the whole family — the children’s choir is a special highlight, and little ones attend free. We would love to see a strong St. Mary presence this Saturday. Come celebrate our faith, culture, and homeland, and help support the Genocide Memorial.
