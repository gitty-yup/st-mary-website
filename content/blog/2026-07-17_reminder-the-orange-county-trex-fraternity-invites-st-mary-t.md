# OC Trex Annual Picnic

Supporting the 49th Annual _**Orange County Trex**_ Picnic

Dear St. Mary Parishioners and Friends,

You are warmly invited to support and attend the **49th Annual Orange County Trex Picnic** on **Sunday, July 19, 2026 at 12:00 noon** at Mason Regional Park in Irvine.

The Triple X / Trex Fraternity has served the Armenian community since 1918, promoting charity, benevolence, education, social culture, and the strengthening of friendship within the Armenian community.

## Event Details

**Date:** Sunday, July 19, 2026

**Time:** 12:00 Noon

**Location:** Mason Regional Park – Area 6

**Address:** 18712 University Drive, Irvine, CA 92612

**Note:** Parking fee required

## Enjoy a Great Community Afternoon

The picnic will include a delicious **shish and chicken kebab lunch**, **live music**, and **kids’ games**. It is a wonderful opportunity for fellowship, family fun, and support of a long-standing Armenian community organization.
