Dear St. Mary Parishioners and Friends,

The Semercian family warmly invites our St. Mary community to join them in prayer as they lovingly remember **Silva Semercian** on the **40th day** of her passing and commemorate the **5th anniversary** of the passing of **Ara Semercian**.

The memorial prayer service will be held on:

**Sunday, August 2, 2026**  
**St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church – Costa Mesa**

Following the Divine Liturgy, a **Hokehankist (Requiem) Service** will be offered at **1:30 PM** at:

**Pacific View Cemetery**  
3500 Pacific View Drive  
Corona del Mar, CA

After the cemetery service, the family warmly invites all parishioners, relatives, and friends to join them for fellowship and to honor the memories of Silva and Ara together.

For additional information, please contact:

-   **Saro:** (714) 200-8838
    
-   **Alin:** (818) 472-1142
    

May God grant rest to the souls of **Silva** and **Ara Semercian**, and may their memory remain eternal.

**With prayers,**

Costa Mesa, California
