date: 2026-05-09

**Dear St. Mary Parishioners and Friends,**

We hope this message finds you well.

It is with regret that the **St. Mary Cultural Committee** **must announce the cancellation of the St. Mary Talent Show** originally scheduled for Sunday, May 17th, 2026 at 1:15 PM in Stambolian Hall. 

**After careful consideration, we have decided to cancel the event.** We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause, especially to those who were preparing to perform or planning to attend. We are grateful for the enthusiasm and support shown by our talented parishioners and community.

We remain committed to creating joyful opportunities for fellowship and celebration of our gifts in the near future. Please stay tuned for upcoming Cultural Committee events.

**If you have any questions**, feel free to contact Nazeli at 714-319-9101.

Thank you for your understanding and continued support of our parish family.

With gratitude and love,

**St. Mary Cultural Committee**
