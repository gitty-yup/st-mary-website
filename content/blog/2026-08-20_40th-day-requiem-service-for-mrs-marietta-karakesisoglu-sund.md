Dear St. Mary Parishioners,  
  
The family of our beloved **Mrs. Marietta Karakesisoglu (1946–2026)** invites the St. Mary parish community to join them in prayer and remembrance at her **40th Day Requiem Service**.  
  
**Sunday, August 23, 2026**  
1:30 p.m.  
  
**Westminster Memorial Park**  
14801 Beach Blvd.  
Westminster, CA 92683  
  
As Marietta’s family gathers to honor her memory and pray for the repose of her soul, they would be grateful for the presence and prayers of our St. Mary community.  
  
May our Lord grant rest to the soul of His departed servant Marietta and bring comfort and consolation to her family and loved ones.  
  
**May her memory be eternal.**  
  
With prayers,  
**St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church**
