**Dear Faithful of St. Mary,**  
We are delighted to announce a new Adult Bible Study that will begin next week facilitated by our very own, Hayr Eremia!

These weekly gatherings are designed as an ongoing exploration of Holy Scripture — a chance to dig deeper into the living Word of God, ask questions, share insights, and grow together in faith. Sessions will last 1 to 1½ hours and will take place at St. Mary Armenian Chruch.

**Two class options are available so more of us can participate:**

**Monday Evenings – 7:00 pm** Ideal for working adults and those with daytime commitments. First session: Monday, August 24

**Wednesday Mornings – 10:30 am** Especially suited for retirees and anyone who prefers not to drive at night. First session: Wednesday, August 26

For the Wednesday morning class, we are happy to help coordinate carpools for those who may need a ride. Simply let us know when you RSVP and we will do our best to connect you with others.

You are welcome to simply show up, but an RSVP is greatly appreciated so we can plan accordingly. Please reply to this email, write to [info@stmaryarmenianchurch.com](mailto:info@stmaryarmenianchurch.com), or leave a message with the church office at (949) 650-8367.

**Questions?** Feel free to contact the church office or send us an email!

Whether you have been reading the Bible for decades or are just beginning to explore it more deeply, you are warmly invited. Come as you are — bring your curiosity, your questions, and an open heart. We look forward to learning and growing together.  
In Christ’s love,

**St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church**
