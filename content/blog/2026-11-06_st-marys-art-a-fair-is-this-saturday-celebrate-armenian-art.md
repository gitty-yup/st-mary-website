ST. MARY ARMENIAN APOSTOLIC CHURCH

ART-A-FAIR EXHIBIT

Celebrating Armenian Art & Supporting Our Church

Dear St. Mary Parishioners,

This is a reminder that you are invited to the **St. Mary Art-A-Fair Exhibit**, a special showcase featuring the works of talented artists from our Armenian community.

Please join us for a reception on **Saturday, November 7, 2026, from 4:00 to 7:00 PM**, as we celebrate Armenian artistry, creativity, and cultural heritage.

This event is also a **special fundraiser benefiting St. Mary Armenian Church**, with artwork available for purchase throughout the exhibit. We are grateful to our participating artists for sharing their talents and helping support our parish.

As you enjoy the beautiful works on display, we hope you will consider taking a piece home with you. Every purchase is more than an appreciation of art—it is an expression of love and support for our church and the community we share. **Your generosity helps St. Mary continue to be a place of faith, fellowship, and Armenian heritage for generations to come.**

Event Details

**Date:** Saturday, November 7, 2026  
**Time:** 4:00 – 7:00 PM  
**Location:** St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church  
148 22nd Street, Costa Mesa, CA 92627

Whether you are an art enthusiast, looking for something special for your home, or simply wish to spend a lovely evening with your parish family, we hope you will join us.

Please invite your family and friends, enjoy the creativity of our Armenian artists, and, if you are able, **consider supporting this fundraiser with a generous purchase.** Every contribution is meaningful, and together we can make this event a wonderful success for St. Mary!

We look forward to sharing this special evening with you!

Warm regards,  
  
**St. Mary Parish Council**
