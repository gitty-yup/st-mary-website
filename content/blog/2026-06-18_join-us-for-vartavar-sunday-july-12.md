**Dear St. Mary Parishioners & Friends,**

We are excited to invite you to our **Vartavar Festival of Water**, celebrating the **Feast of the Transfiguration of our Lord** on **Sunday, July 12, 2026**.

The celebration will begin **immediately after Holy Badarak** in the **Hachigian Courtyard**. Come ready to honor this joyful Armenian tradition of faith, community, and celebration! We will unite our ancient heritage with our Christian faith through water, roses, and blessings.

**A friendly heads-up:** If you participate in the water festivities, you _will_ get wet—possibly very wet! This is all part of the fun and the spirit of Vartavar. We encourage everyone to **bring a change of clothes and towels** to fully enjoy the day.

Pizza & refreshments will be available for purchase. Everyone is invited to be refreshed in body and spirit as we come together as a parish to celebrate!

![](/media/uploads/newsletters/join-us-for-vartavar-sunday-july-12_1.png)

Follow this link [https://www.stmaryarmenianchurch.com/vartavar](https://www.stmaryarmenianchurch.com/vartavar) to learn more about the Armenian Apostolic Tradition of Vartavar and the associated Feast of the Transfiguration!

We look forward to celebrating with you!

In Christ,  
**St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church**
