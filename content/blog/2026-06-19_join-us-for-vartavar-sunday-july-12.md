Dear St. Mary Parishioners and Friends,

You are warmly invited to our **Vartavar — Festival of Water**, celebrating the **Feast of the Transfiguration** on **Sunday, July 12, 2026**!

The festivities begin **immediately after Holy Badarak** in the **Hachigian Courtyard**.

A friendly warning: you **will get wet — possibly very wet!** Be sure to bring extra clothing and towels. **Pizza and refreshments** will be available for purchase.

Come celebrate one of Armenia's most beloved and joyful summer traditions with your parish family — a day of water, light, laughter, and faith.

[**Learn more about Vartavar →**](https://www.stmaryarmenianchurch.com/vartavar)

We look forward to celebrating with you!

In Christ,\
**St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church**
