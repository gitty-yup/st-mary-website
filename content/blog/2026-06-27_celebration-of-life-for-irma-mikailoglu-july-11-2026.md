Dear St. Mary Parishioners and Friends,

It is with a heavy heart that we share the news of the passing of our beloved parishioner and friend, Irma Mikailoglu. Irma touched many lives through her warmth, her love of fashion, and her generous spirit. She will be deeply missed by our community.

The family invites you to join us for a **Celebration of Life** to honor and remember Irma:

**Date:** Saturday, July 11, 2026

**Prayer Service** 1:00 – 1:45 PM Pacific View Mortuary 3500 Pacific View Drive Corona Del Mar, CA 92625

**Reception Following** 2:00 – 4:00 PM Zov’s Restaurant 21123 Newport Coast Drive Newport Beach, CA 92657

Please join us for hors d’oeuvres and desserts as we honor and celebrate our beautiful Irma. We encourage you to bring your favorite stories and memories to share.

In Irma’s honor, we suggest wearing casual, colorful, fashionable attire — especially if it’s something you acquired from her store.

May God grant Irma eternal rest and comfort her family and all who loved her.

With prayers and sympathy,  
**St. Mary Parish Council**
