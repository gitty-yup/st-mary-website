“Honor the Lord with your wealth and with the firstfruits of all your produce; then your barns will be filled with plenty.”\
— Proverbs 3:9–10

Dear Faithful,

On **Sunday, August 16, 2026**, St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church of Costa Mesa will observe the Feast of the Assumption of the Holy Mother of God—one of the five major Feast Days of the Armenian Church—celebrated on the Sunday nearest August 15.

As Holy Scripture calls us to honor the Lord with the firstfruits of our labor, our Church blesses the grapes after Divine Liturgy. The grape holds a place of honor among all fruits, for from it comes the wine used in the Sacraments of the Divine Liturgy, Holy Communion, and Holy Matrimony.

**Madagh** is an act of mercy, thanksgiving, and almsgiving. On major feasts and our Church name day, the faithful offer Madagh in gratitude to God for health and well-being, and to beseech spiritual peace from the Almighty.

Please make your heartfelt and tax-deductible Name-Day/Madagh donation using one of the options below. Your generosity strengthens our parish mission and those in need.

Dear St. Mary Parishioners,

What if you never had to search for a parking space at St. Mary again?

For the first time, St. Mary is auctioning **FOUR exclusive reserved parking spaces**—and one of them could have **YOUR NAME** on it!

**Only four spaces are available.**

Bidding will take place online, so you can participate right from your phone, tablet, or computer. And don’t worry if you can’t be with us in person on Sunday—you can continue bidding from wherever you are.

Starting Bid: $500

[BID ON YOUR RESERVED PARKING SPACE](https://smaac.betterworld.org/auctions/st-mary-reserved-parking-auction)

Whether you’re bidding for the convenience, the fun of seeing your family’s name on your own parking space, or simply as a special way to support St. Mary, we hope you’ll join in!

Bidding opens today at 10:00 PM!
