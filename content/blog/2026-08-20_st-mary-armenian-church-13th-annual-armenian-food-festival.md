Dear St. Mary Armenian Church Parish Family,

We are excited to invite you to participate in the **St. Mary Armenian Church 13th Annual Armenian Food Festival!**

This special event is much more than a festival—it is an opportunity for us to come together as a parish family and share the beauty and richness of our Armenian heritage with our local Orange County community. Through **Armenian music, dance, performance, traditions, food and drink**, we have a unique opportunity to introduce our culture to our non-Armenian neighbors, friends, and community members.

**The success of this festival depends on all of us.** We need the participation and support of our entire parish family to make this year’s festival a memorable and successful celebration of our Armenian culture.

There are several important ways you can help:

**Volunteer Your Time**  
We need parishioners of all ages to volunteer before, during, and after the festival. Whether you can help with food preparation, serving, hospitality, setup, cleanup, or other festival activities, every helping hand makes a difference.

**Spread the Word**  
Please share the festival info and the [Festival](https://d8c4671e-41b1-4617-91fc-06609ef95b8e.usrfiles.com/ugd/d8c467_dee026ff5809460abd4650df0ec61e58.pdf) [Flyer](https://d8c4671e-41b1-4617-91fc-06609ef95b8e.usrfiles.com/ugd/d8c467_dee026ff5809460abd4650df0ec61e58.pdf) with your family, friends, coworkers, neighbors, and anyone in our local community who would enjoy experiencing Armenian culture. Encourage them to attend and invite others to join us!

**Bring Your Family & Friends**  
We especially encourage our parish families to attend together and bring guests. This is one of the few opportunities each year when we can open our doors and share our Armenian traditions and hospitality with the broader Orange County community.
