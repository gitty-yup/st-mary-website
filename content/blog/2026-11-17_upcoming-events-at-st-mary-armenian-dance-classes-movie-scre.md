Dear St. Mary Parishioners,

As we approach the holiday season, we are pleased to share several upcoming opportunities to come together in fellowship, celebrate our Armenian heritage, and enjoy time with family and friends.

We encourage you to participate in these wonderful events and support the organizations that help keep our community vibrant and connected.  
 

* * *

###   
🇦🇲 Traditional Armenian Dance Classes

**Presented by the St. Mary Cultural Committee**

Join us for four Friday evenings of traditional Armenian dance! Whether you're looking to learn something new, reconnect with our cultural traditions, or simply enjoy an evening with fellow parishioners, everyone ages 12 and up is welcome.

-   **Beginning:** Friday, November 20, 2026
    
-   **Time:** 7:30 PM – 9:30 PM
    
-   **Duration:** 4 weeks (Friday evenings)
    
-   **Location:** Barsam Hall, St. Mary Armenian Church
    
-   **Cost:** $20 | **FREE for ACYO members**
    
-   **Instructor:** Arsen Gueleserian
    

Come learn, dance, and help keep our beautiful Armenian traditions alive!

###   
🎬 _A Winter's Song_ — Special Movie Screening

**Presented by Hamazkayin Orange County Siamanto Chapter**

Join our Armenian community for a special screening of _A Winter's Song_, followed by a question-and-answer session with director and screenwriter Angela Asatrian and actress Krista Marina.

-   **Date:** Sunday, December 6, 2026
    
-   **Time:** 4:00 PM
    
-   **Location:** Ghazarian Hall, Harut Barsamian Armenian Center
    
-   **Address:** 5305 W. McFadden Avenue, Santa Ana, CA
    
-   **Admission:** FREE
    

This is a wonderful opportunity to support Armenian filmmaking and enjoy an afternoon of cinema and conversation. If you haven't seen _A Winter's Song_ yet, you're in for a treat. If you have, this is a unique opportunity to connect with those who created and star in it.
