**Dear St. Mary Parishioners and Friends,**  
  
Our parishioners and community members play a vital role in shaping the life and future of our Armenian Church community in the Western Diocese. By sharing your experiences, perspectives, and ideas, you help strengthen our parishes and support the spiritual and cultural life of our community.  
  
We invite you to take part in this **important survey** designed to better understand the experiences, beliefs, and engagement of Armenian Christians in our parish and community. Your feedback will help us learn how our church is serving the wider community and how we can better support parishioners in their spiritual life, participation, and connection with the Church.  
  
By completing this survey, you will help inform future parish programs, ministries, community initiatives, and opportunities for engagement. Your participation is a valuable personal contribution to the continued growth and vitality of our church community.  
  
The **survey is** **anonymous and confidential**, and it takes approximately **10–15 minutes** to complete. If you are **18 years or older, consider yourself an Armenian Christian,** we urge you to complete the survey. Please take a few moments to share your thoughts by clicking the link or the image below:  
  
**[www.wdcme.com/survey](http://www.wdcme.com/survey)**

![](/media/uploads/newsletters/your-voice-matters-please-take-the-western-diocese-survey_1.jpg)

We warmly encourage every adult in our parish to participate. When St. Mary’s voice is heard, our Diocese is better equipped to support the faith and fellowship we are building together for the next generation.  
  
Thank you for your time and for the active role you play in the life of our church. Your feedback is a true gift to our community.  
  
With gratitude and in Christ’s service,

**Mike Hollis**  
Parish Council Chairman  
St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church
