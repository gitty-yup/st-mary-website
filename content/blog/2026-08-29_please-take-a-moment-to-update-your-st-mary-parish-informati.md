St. Mary Armenian Apostolic Church

Parish Records Update

 

Dear St. Mary Family,

Staying connected as a parish begins with something very simple: **making sure we have the correct information for you and your family.**

Accurate parish records help St. Mary communicate important announcements, invitations, sacramental and parish information, special events, stewardship communications, and other updates that are part of the life of our church community. They also help us maintain an accurate picture of the families and individuals who make up our parish.

We are asking **every St. Mary parishioner and household** to take just a few minutes to review the information we currently have on file and make any necessary corrections.

[Review & Update My Information](https://www.stmaryarmenianchurch.com/update-my-info)

## How It Works

First, visit the parish records page and enter your **email address**. You will then receive an email containing a **secure, personalized link** to your parish record.

Click that link to see the information St. Mary currently has on file for you and make any necessary updates.

## What Should You Review?

Please carefully check and update, as needed:

-   First, Middle and Last Name
-   Preferred Name
-   Email Address
-   Phone Number
-   Mailing Address
-   Apartment or Unit Number, if applicable
-   City, State, ZIP Code and Country

**Even if you believe we already have the correct information, please take a moment to review it.** What appears on the form is what is currently contained in our parish records.
